ℹ️ How it works

The lens is stepped from near to far focus and a frame is grabbed at each step. When you tap a point, the app measures local sharpness (Sobel gradient energy) in a small window around it in every frame. The frame where that spot is sharpest tells you where the lens was focused. That focus distance is the distance to the point. The peak is refined with a Gaussian fit in diopters (1/m). Accuracy is best up close and drops fast with distance, because depth of field grows. Pick spots with texture or edges; flat, featureless areas give weak readings.
